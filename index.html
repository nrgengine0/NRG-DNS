Installation Guide

1 Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2 Pick a DNS server and tap Start DNS.
3 When iOS asks permission to download the profile, tap Allow.
4 Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded.
5 Select NRG DNS, tap Install and confirm.
6 Verify under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
